No model is asked. Each command with a `keep` in corpus.toml lists facts its answer needs, taken from its raw output: a chosen set, not all an answer could use. An output keeps a fact when one of its lines holds it whole (no letter, digit, `_`, `.` or `-` just before it, no letter, digit or `_` just after it, though a short hash may run on into more hex digits), whitespace aside, and a fact the raw output has n times must be held n times. A fact an output reworded counts as lost, so read the lost facts before reading a loss as a defect. A way to recover is recognised by each tool's hint text, not tried.
